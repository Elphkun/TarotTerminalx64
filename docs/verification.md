## Notes (checked against Hulse and confirmed by the author, 2026-11-19)

- **Judgement / Shin** is attributed to Fire, with Spirit kept as `secondary_attribution`. `dignity_element` is `fire`.
- **Planetary trumps** take their dignity element from `planet_elements` (by planetary nature): Sun and Mars fire, Moon and Venus water, Mercury and Jupiter air, Saturn earth. The World as Earth agrees with Hulse, who gives it as the elemental throne of the Page of Pentacles. The others are your choice, not from Hulse.
- **Page of Pentacles** is on Hulse's 48th path, the last invisible path, which joins no two sephiroth, so `connects` is null by design.
- **Court spans and decans** were checked against Hulse (pp. 309-317) for all 12 courts with a span: every span, decan card and predominant sign matched. Titles, family roles, thrones and invisible paths 33-48 are taken from the same pages.
- **Hulse's Knight of Swords** reads "the elemental counterchange of Fire of Water": a misprint or OCR error for Fire of Air. The data keeps Fire of Air.
- **Pip titles where Hulse's wording differs**: he calls the Six of Swords "Science" (Crowley's title) and the Three of Pentacles "Material Work". The data keeps the Book T titles "Lord of Earned Success" and "Lord of Material Works".
- **Court `dignity_element`** is the suit element (e.g. Knight of Cups → water), not the sub-element. Change any card if you read it differently.
- **Sephirothic planets of Kether, Chokmah and Malkuth** are stored as `primum_mobile`, `zodiac` and `elements`. Sources word these differently.
- **GD titles** for trumps, pips and courts were written out from the Book T titles in common use; check the spelling and punctuation (e.g. "Illusionary Success", "Sun of the Morning" for the Emperor, "Spirit of Aether" for the Fool).
- **Hebrew letter spellings** (`cheth`, `tzaddi`, `qoph`, `heh`, `vav`) and the one-word letter meanings in `correspondences.json` are a common transliteration; change them to match Hulse if you prefer his.
- **Decans** follow the Chaldean order starting with Mars at 0° Aries (2 of Wands). The 36 decans are checked as unique, and each sign is checked to belong to the suit's element. The planet assigned to each decan still needs to be checked by eye.
- **Pip `decan.degrees`** and trump `secondary_attribution` are small additions beyond the schema in CLAUDE.md.
